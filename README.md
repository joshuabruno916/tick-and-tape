# 🧾 Tick & Tape

A free app for accountants that marks up PDFs the way you mark up paper: on-page calculations, tick marks, stamps, tie-outs, and review notes. Use it at **[tt.smf916.com](https://tt.smf916.com)**. It runs entirely in your browser, and your files never leave your computer.

**Version 1.0.4** · October 2026


## ✨ What it does

- 🧮 **Tapes.** An adding-machine tape beside the page. Key amounts, or click them on the PDF and a tick drops beside each one.
- ✔️ **Ticks, stamps and notes.** ENTERED, REVIEWED, AGREES, NON TAX, PY, N/A and DUPLICATE, plus shapes, highlights and text. A tick-mark legend puts the firm's meaning for each mark on the workpaper.
- 🔗 **Tie-outs.** Link an amount to where it goes. The app checks that the two agree.
- 📝 **Review pass.** Numbered review notes (R1, R2…) with Open, Answered and Cleared. Save a clean final copy without them.
- 📋 **Markup summary.** An optional last page that lists every tape, tie-out, note, stamp and tick mark with its pages, each drawn in its own color.
- 📄 **Page tools.** Rotate, delete, reorder, extract and add pages, plus bookmarks. Open several PDFs at once, such as a year of bank statements, to combine them into one, in date order.
- 📝 **Forms.** Type into a PDF's own fill-in boxes, such as a client organizer or a W-9. Every PDF reader shows what you typed, and a flattened copy makes it part of the page.
- 🔍 **Scans.** Make a scanned PDF searchable, then pick its amounts like any others. Amounts the app isn't sure of are marked **check**.
- 📊 **Excel.** Paste cells in as tape lines, and copy a tape back out as rows.
- 🖼️ **Pictures.** Paste a screenshot straight onto a page.
- ⬛ **Redaction.** Mark Social Security numbers, account numbers and other private details, then save a redacted copy. The text under each black box is removed from the file, not just covered.


## 🚀 Using it

Open **[tt.smf916.com](https://tt.smf916.com)** in Chrome or Edge. Those two can save back to the same file; other browsers download a copy instead.

To install it as an app, click **Install as an app** on the opening screen, or choose **Install** in the browser's address bar or menu. It gets its own window and a Start menu entry, works offline, can open two PDFs side by side in two windows, and updates itself when a new version comes out.

New to it? Click **Try the sample** to practice on 30 made-up pages, or read the **[user guide](https://tt.smf916.com/guide.html)** (the **?** button in the app opens it too). It walks through everything, with pictures, and works offline too once the app is installed (Chromium-based browsers only — Chrome, Edge, Brave, etc.).


## 🔒 Privacy

No server, no uploads, no outside connections. Scans are read on your own computer. The app downloads only its own files from this site, and PDFs never leave the computer. Unsaved work is kept as an encrypted draft that only the same PDF can open.
